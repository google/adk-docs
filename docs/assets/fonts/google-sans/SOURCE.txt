Google Sans web fonts
=====================

These variable WOFF2 files contain the Latin and Latin Extended subsets of
Google Sans, in normal and italic styles with a supported weight range of
400–700.

Project: https://github.com/googlefonts/googlesans
Web font source: https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Google+Sans
Google Fonts release path: fonts.gstatic.com/s/googlesans/v71/
